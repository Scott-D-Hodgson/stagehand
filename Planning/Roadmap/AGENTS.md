# Roadmap guidance

The roadmap is a high-level, dependency-informed sequence, not a task checklist or date commitment. Phase detail belongs in the phase folders; implementation-sized work and estimates belong in `Planning/Work Plan/` or the backlog.

- Keep phase outcomes and dependencies understandable without duplicating every story.
- Preserve the separation between Phase 1–4 and `Later-candidates`; do not silently promote later ideas into scope.
- Link phase outcomes to relevant backlog items and work-plan milestones where useful.
- When priorities, dependencies, or scope change, state the reason and keep related documents consistent.
- Avoid adding calendar dates or delivery promises unless the project owner supplies them.
