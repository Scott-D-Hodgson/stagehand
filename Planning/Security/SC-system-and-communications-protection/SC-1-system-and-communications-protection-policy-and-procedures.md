# SC-1 - System and Communications Protection Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SC-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a316sc1).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute system/communications protection policy and procedures to assigned roles; review and update them at defined intervals.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipient roles and policy/procedure review frequency. **Suggestion for review:** involve network, system, security, and service owners; review annually and after major architecture changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy and procedures support selected SC-family controls, reflect applicable government requirements, and may be organisation-wide or system-specific based on risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like the rules for how doors, corridors, and phone lines in a building are protected and maintained.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand maintainers should document product network behaviour and security assumptions when implemented. Operators establish network policies covering runner egress, target access, remote administration, and any externally hosted editor or service.
