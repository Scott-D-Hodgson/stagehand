# SC-101 - Unclassified Telecommunications Systems in Secure Facilities

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SC-101](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a316sc101).

## Control definition

In secure facilities, configure unclassified telecommunications so idle systems do not transmit sensitive audio and cannot be externally activated; protect telephone systems from exploitation and unauthorized changes; apply on-hook audio protection, isolation, controlled administration, no external remote maintenance, restrictions on speakerphones/conferencing, secure messaging/answering features, physical/electrical separation from classified systems, and applicable VoIP installation requirements.

## Supplemental guidance

A Secure Facility is a physical security zone processing classified information. The entry references CNSSI requirements for on-hook audio protection, computerized telephone systems, and VoIP.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like ensuring an ordinary phone in a secure room cannot quietly act as a microphone or bridge into classified communications.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This control is wholly facility/telecommunications-specific and is not an application feature. Operators in secure facilities must follow CSE/security-authority requirements for telephony installation, separation, maintenance, and exceptions; Stagehand cannot validate facility compliance.
