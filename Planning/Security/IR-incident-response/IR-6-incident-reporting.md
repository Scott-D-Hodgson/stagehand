# IR-6 — Incident Reporting

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IR-6](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Require personnel to report suspected incidents to the response capability within a defined period and report incident information to designated authorities.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** reporting time and authorities. **Suggestion for review:** require immediate reporting of suspected credential/data exposure and set other deadlines from organisational policy and applicable obligations.

## Supplemental guidance

Report types, content, timing, and recipients should follow applicable government and organisational requirements. Suspicious communications can themselves be reportable events.

## Plain-language metaphor

When you smell smoke, call the fire service promptly and give them the location and what you observed.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Provide a clear route for Stagehand users to report unintended actions, disclosure, suspicious plans, or compromised credentials. Operators should include deployment and target-service details and notify providers where agreements require it. Share only necessary information with external parties.

## Enhancements

1. **IR-6(1), automated reporting:** use mechanisms that assist incident reporting.
2. **IR-6(2), vulnerabilities related to incidents:** report associated vulnerabilities to defined roles.
3. **IR-6(3), supply-chain coordination:** provide relevant incident information to supply-chain organisations, balancing response value against disclosure risk.
