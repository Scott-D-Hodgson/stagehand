# IR-2 — Incident Response Training

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IR-2](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a38ir2).

## Control definition

Train personnel for their incident-response duties within an organisation-defined period after assuming those duties, when system changes require it, and at a defined recurring frequency.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** initial training period and recurring frequency. **Suggestion for review:** train before an individual joins response rotation; refresh annually and after material response-plan changes.

## Supplemental guidance

Training detail should fit the role: users identify/report, administrators contain/remediate, and responders handle investigation, forensics, recovery, and restoration. Include suspicious activity from internal and external sources.

## Plain-language metaphor

People practice the emergency job they are actually expected to do, not just read the fire plan once.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Train users to stop automation safely and report unexpected actions or data exposure. Train administrators/responders to isolate runners, revoke credentials, preserve approved evidence, inspect logs, and rebuild from trusted sources. Do not train users to investigate sensitive incidents outside their role.

## Enhancements

1. **IR-2(1), simulated events:** use incident scenarios to practice response under crisis conditions.
2. **IR-2(2), automated training environments:** use automation to provide a realistic practice environment.
