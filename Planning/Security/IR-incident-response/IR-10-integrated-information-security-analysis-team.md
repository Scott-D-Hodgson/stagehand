# IR-10 — Integrated Information Security Analysis Team

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IR-10](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Establish an integrated information-security analysis team to support incident analysis and response across organisational disciplines.

## Supplemental guidance

The catalogue’s intent is integrated analysis that brings together relevant technical and organisational expertise. Use the official entry and organisational incident-response model to tailor membership and operation.

## Plain-language metaphor

A complicated incident needs a coordinated crew—technical responders, business owners, and specialists—not a single person working from one clue.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

The adopting organisation should involve application owners, endpoint/browser administrators, identity/network teams, incident responders, privacy/legal representatives, and relevant service providers when Stagehand is within an incident’s scope. Stagehand itself is not an organisational response team.
