# IR-1 — Incident Response Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IR-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a38ir1).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute incident-response policy/procedures to defined roles; cover purpose, scope, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance; review/update them on defined schedules; and support heightened readiness during emergency or elevated IT-threat situations.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipients and review frequencies. **Suggestion for review:** include responders, system owners, operators, and management; review annually and after incidents/exercises or significant changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy establishes how the organisation implements the IR family and should reflect applicable legislation and government direction. Enterprise policy may be supplemented with system-specific procedures based on risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

This is the emergency playbook: everyone knows who calls whom, what to protect first, and how to act when conditions worsen.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Include Stagehand deployments in incident procedures for credential exposure, unexpected browser actions, sensitive data disclosure, compromised runner/dependency, or suspicious automation. Define safe-stop, evidence preservation, escalation, and recovery roles. Stagehand documentation is not the adopting organisation’s incident policy.
