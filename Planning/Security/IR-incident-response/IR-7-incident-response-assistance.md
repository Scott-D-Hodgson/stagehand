# IR-7 — Incident Response Assistance

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IR-7](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a38ir7).

## Control definition

Provide an incident-response support resource within the organisational response capability to advise and assist users with handling and reporting incidents.

## Supplemental guidance

Support can include a help desk, assistance group, or forensic services when needed.

## Plain-language metaphor

Give staff a staffed help line for emergencies instead of leaving them to guess what to do.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

The product should identify where users of an adopting organisation can seek help, but the organisation supplies the actual response channel. Operators should provide support contacts for the runner, identity provider, cloud/CI service, and target systems and clarify escalation hours and evidence handling.

## Enhancements

1. **IR-7(1), automated availability of information/support:** make response instructions and support available through automated push/pull channels.
2. **IR-7(2), coordination with external providers:** establish a cooperative relationship with security providers and identify response team members to them.
