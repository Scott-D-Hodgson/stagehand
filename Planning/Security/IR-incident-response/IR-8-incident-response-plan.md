# IR-8 — Incident Response Plan

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IR-8](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Maintain a plan describing response capability structure, organisational integration, reportable incidents, metrics, resources, and management support; obtain approval; distribute it to defined responders; review/update it; communicate changes; and protect it from unauthorized disclosure or alteration.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** approvers, recipients, review frequency, incident definitions, and metrics. **Suggestion for review:** review annually and after exercises/incidents; provide controlled access to named responders and roles.

## Supplemental guidance

Use a coordinated approach tailored to mission, size, structure, and functions. Include information-sharing coordination with external service and supply-chain providers as appropriate.

## Plain-language metaphor

This is the map for the response team: roles, routes, triggers, measures, and contacts, stored where only the right people can change it.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Include the Stagehand deployment boundary, incident classes, safe-stop steps, evidence sources, credential revocation, provider contacts, and recovery checks. Protect the plan because it reveals response processes and system details. Keep a separate product-maintainer process for vulnerabilities affecting all users.
