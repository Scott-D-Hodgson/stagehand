# IR-9 — Information Spillage Response

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IR-9](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

For information spills, identify affected information; alert designated roles using a communication channel not involved in the spill; isolate contaminated systems/components; eradicate the information; identify further contamination; and take other defined actions.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** alert roles and additional response actions; assigned spill-response personnel, training frequency, and post-spill continuity steps in enhancements.

## Supplemental guidance

A spill occurs when classified or sensitive information is placed on a system not authorized to process it. Response depends on sensitivity, system safeguards, affected media, and user access. Use an independent communication channel to avoid spreading the material further.

## Plain-language metaphor

If paint spills onto a surface where it does not belong, cordon off the area, tell the cleanup team through a clean route, and check what else was touched.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

If sensitive data enters an unauthorized browser, plan, screenshot, trace, or log, stop automation, isolate affected storage, avoid forwarding the artifact, and contact the designated security team through an approved channel. Preserve only what responders direct. Operators should identify copies in caches, backups, CI logs, and provider systems; never attempt ad hoc deletion that could destroy evidence.

## Enhancements

1. **IR-9(1), responsible personnel:** assign defined spill-response roles.
2. **IR-9(2), training:** provide spill-response training at a defined frequency.
3. **IR-9(3), post-spill operations:** keep affected personnel able to perform assigned work while systems are remediated.
4. **IR-9(4), exposure to unauthorized personnel:** apply defined safeguards for people exposed to information outside their authorization.
