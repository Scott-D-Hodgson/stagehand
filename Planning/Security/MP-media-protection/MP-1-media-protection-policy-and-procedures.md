# MP-1 — Media Protection Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, MP-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a310mp1).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute media-protection policy/procedures to defined roles; cover purpose, scope, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance; and review/update on defined schedules.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipients and review frequencies. **Suggestion for review:** include users handling exports, removable media, reports, and backups; review annually and after data-handling changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy supports MP controls and reflects applicable government requirements. Organisation-wide rules may be supplemented by system-specific procedures based on risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

These are the rules for labelling, carrying, storing, and disposing of boxes that may contain sensitive papers.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Treat plans, downloads, screenshots, traces, logs, backups, and exported reports as media that may contain sensitive information. Operators should define approved storage, transport, marking, retention, sanitization, and sharing practices for each artifact type.
