# MP-3 — Media Marking

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, MP-3](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a310mp3).

## Control definition

Mark information-system media indicating distribution limitations, handling caveats, and applicable security markings.

## Supplemental guidance

Marking helps people recognize required handling. Apply organisation-defined classification/handling rules to media and associated outputs, taking account of both visible labels and machine-readable metadata where applicable.

## Plain-language metaphor

Put the right handling label on a parcel so every person who receives it knows where it may go and how it must be protected.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Reports, screenshots, traces, plans, and downloads may require labels based on their contents. Operators should define markings and handling templates and verify exports retain them. Stagehand’s automated classification/marking is not claimed; AC-15 is withdrawn into this control.
