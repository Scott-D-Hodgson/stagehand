# MP-5 — Media Transport

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, MP-5](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a310mp5).

## Control definition

Protect and control digital and non-digital media during transport outside controlled areas and maintain accountability for media movement.

## Supplemental guidance

Transport safeguards include packaging, custody, approved carriers, and protections against loss or disclosure. Choose safeguards according to information sensitivity and transport mode.

## Plain-language metaphor

When carrying a sealed package between buildings, keep track of who has it and use a protected route.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Use approved encrypted transfer channels for plans, logs, screenshots, and reports; avoid personal email or consumer file-sharing. Keep transfer and recipient records where required and remove local copies after approved transfer.
