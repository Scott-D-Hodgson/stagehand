# MP-2 — Media Access

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, MP-2](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a310mp2).

## Control definition

Restrict access to digital and non-digital media containing organisational information to authorized individuals.

## Supplemental guidance

Media access restrictions include electronic media such as disks, USB devices, tapes, and portable drives, and non-digital forms such as paper. Apply the safeguards appropriate to the medium and information.

## Plain-language metaphor

Lock the filing cabinet and the USB drawer; information needs protection whether printed or stored electronically.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Restrict access to plans, logs, screenshots, traces, downloaded files, and exported reports. Use least-privilege storage permissions, secure artifact repositories, and controlled removable media. Verify CI artifacts and browser profiles are not publicly accessible.
