# AU-13 — Monitoring for Information Disclosure

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, AU-13](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a33au13).

## Control definition

Monitor organisation-defined public/open-source information sites at an organisation-defined frequency for signs that organisational information was disclosed without authorization.

**Organisation-defined parameters to review:** sites or sources and monitoring frequency. **Suggested starting point:** monitor approved public repositories, organisational web properties, and relevant disclosure channels on a risk-based cadence, with urgent alerts for high-risk data matches.

## Supplemental guidance

Open sources can include public social-networking sites. Monitoring should be scoped to identify unauthorized disclosure and performed under applicable privacy and legal requirements.

## In plain language

Check public noticeboards and websites to see whether private papers have accidentally been posted.

## Stagehand initial assessment

Stagehand is not assessed as a public-disclosure monitoring service. Organisations should use approved monitoring tools and response processes. Where automation is used for monitoring, restrict its sources, credentials, collection, and retention; have trained staff validate alerts before response.

## Control enhancements

1. **AU-13(1), automated tools:** use automated mechanisms to identify possible unauthorized disclosure.
2. **AU-13(2), review monitored sites:** revisit the selected monitoring sources at an organisation-defined frequency.
