# AU-1 — Audit and Accountability Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, AU-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute an audit and accountability policy and implementing procedures to designated roles. The policy covers purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance. Review and update both on organisation-defined schedules.

**Organisation-defined parameters to review:** recipients and review frequency for policy and procedures. **Suggested values for review:** distribute to system owners, administrators, security/audit roles, and other affected users; review annually and after material changes or incidents.

## Supplemental guidance

These policies establish how the organisation implements the Audit and Accountability family. They should reflect applicable legislation and government direction, may be integrated into enterprise policy, and may be supplemented with system-specific procedures according to risk.

## In plain language

This is the organisation’s rulebook for keeping activity records: it says what gets recorded, who checks it, and how the process stays current.

## Stagehand initial assessment

Stagehand’s repository and deployment logs have not been validated here as an audit capability. Organisations should define audit responsibilities across Stagehand, browser, identity, host, and target-service layers; document which layer records each event; and establish review and escalation procedures. Do not assume product debug logs provide complete or durable security auditing.
