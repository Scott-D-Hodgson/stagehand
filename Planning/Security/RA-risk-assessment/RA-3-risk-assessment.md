# RA-3 — Risk Assessment

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, RA-3](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Assess likelihood and impact of unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction; document and distribute results; review and update on schedule and after significant changes.

Suggested review: annual reassessment and whenever architecture, data, threats, or deployment changes materially; distribute to system owner, security authority, and authorizer.

## Supplemental guidance

Assessment covers threats, vulnerabilities, internal and external parties, and impacts at organisational, business, or system levels.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like checking both how likely a hazard is and how bad the damage would be.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This initial assessment is not a formal organisation risk assessment. Operators should assess the runner, browser privileges, target systems, data in plans and artifacts, CI, and hosting boundaries.

## Control enhancements

None listed.

## Assessment status

**Planned / not verified.** Stagehand implementation and deployment evidence must be assessed before assigning an implemented or compliant status. This summary is a paraphrase for planning, not an authorization decision.
