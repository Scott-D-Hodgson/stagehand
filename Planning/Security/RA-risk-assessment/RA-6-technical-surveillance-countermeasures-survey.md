# RA-6 - Technical Surveillance Countermeasures Survey

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, RA-6](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a314ra6).

## Control definition

Use qualified personnel to conduct technical surveillance countermeasures surveys at assigned locations on a chosen schedule and/or when defined events or indicators occur.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** locations, frequency, triggering events/indicators. **Suggestion for review:** scope only facilities designated by the security authority and set cadence based on facility risk and applicable policy.

## Supplemental guidance

Qualified personnel examine facilities visually, electronically, and physically to detect surveillance devices/hazards and technical weaknesses that could aid technical penetration. Findings can inform risk assessments and exposure analysis.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like asking a qualified inspector to check a sensitive room for hidden microphones or other ways someone could listen in.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This is a facility security activity, not a Stagehand product feature. Operators handling sensitive information should consult their facility security authority and use qualified survey providers where required. Do not treat software scans as a substitute.
