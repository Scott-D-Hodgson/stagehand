# RA-1 — Risk Assessment Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, RA-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a314ra1).

## Control definition

Create, document, distribute, and periodically review risk-assessment policy and procedures for defined roles.

Suggested review: distribute to system owners, security, and risk decision-makers; review annually and after major changes.

## Supplemental guidance

Policy supports RA controls, reflects applicable government requirements, and can be organisation-wide or system-specific.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand’s assessment is a planning aid only. Operators need their own risk process, system boundary, threat assumptions, and approval roles.
