# MA-1 — System Maintenance Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, MA-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute maintenance policy/procedures to defined roles; address purpose, scope, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance; and review/update both on defined schedules.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipients and review cadence. **Suggestion for review:** include system owners, administrators, support personnel, and security staff; review annually and after significant changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy supports MA-family controls, reflects applicable government requirements, and may use enterprise procedures supplemented for specific systems based on risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

This is the maintenance handbook for the workshop: what can be serviced, by whom, and how the work is recorded.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Define maintenance responsibilities for Stagehand releases, dependency/browser updates, runner images, and host patching. Operators should schedule maintenance, coordinate outages, verify security controls afterward, and protect data on devices sent for off-site repair.
