# SI-1 - System and Information Integrity Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SI-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a317si1).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute integrity policy/procedures to assigned roles, and review/update them on defined schedules.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipients and review frequency. **Suggestion for review:** include security, development, operations, and system owners; review annually and after major incidents or system changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy supports selected SI controls, reflects applicable GC/TBS requirements, and may be enterprise-wide or system-specific based on risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like a maintenance rulebook that says how to spot damage, report it, and keep records trustworthy.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Maintainers should define product-level flaw, integrity, validation, and output-handling practices. Operators need policies for host monitoring, patching, malware protection, and artifact handling; the application does not replace them.
