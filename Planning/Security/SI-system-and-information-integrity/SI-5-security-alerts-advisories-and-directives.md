# SI-5 - Security Alerts, Advisories, and Directives

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SI-5](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Receive security alerts/advisories/directives from defined external organisations continuously; issue internal notices as needed; distribute them to assigned internal/external recipients; and implement directives within established timeframes or report the extent of non-compliance.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** external sources and recipients. **Suggestion for review:** monitor Cyber Centre and relevant vendor advisories; route to product/security and deployment owners; track applicability and remediation decisions.

## Supplemental guidance

Timely compliance matters because delays can create immediate adverse effects. External recipients can include partners, suppliers, and service providers.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like a weather warning network that sends the alert to everyone who needs to secure their part of the building.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Maintainers should monitor dependency and runtime security notices and publish actionable release advisories. Operators should subscribe to OS, browser, CI, and Stagehand notices and apply or document exceptions under SI-2.

## Control enhancements

(1) automate availability/distribution of alerts and advisories throughout the organisation.
