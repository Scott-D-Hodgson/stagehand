# AC-21 — Information Sharing

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, AC-21](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Enable authorized users to determine whether access to information is permitted by the applicable information-sharing agreements and associated access-control policies.

**Organisation-defined parameters to review:** information-sharing agreements and policy sources; users or roles allowed to make sharing decisions; and the rules or attributes used to determine permitted recipients.

## Supplemental guidance

Information sharing can cross organisational boundaries. Access decisions should reflect agreements, applicable laws and policy, and data-owner direction. User discretion must operate within those constraints; it is not blanket permission to disclose.

## In plain language

Before lending a book, check the lending rules and who is allowed to borrow it. Being able to hand it over does not mean you are allowed to.

## Stagehand initial assessment

Automation may copy, summarize, upload, or otherwise expose page content. Stagehand should not make independent legal or policy judgments about sharing. Operators should restrict destinations, browser profiles, downloads, clipboard access, and integrations; train users to verify recipient authorization; and place approval or data-loss-prevention checks around transfers of protected information. Confirm that task prompts cannot override the organisation’s sharing agreements.

## Control enhancements

No enhancements are listed for this control in the Annex 3A catalogue. The base control still requires organisation-specific sharing agreements and policy implementation.
