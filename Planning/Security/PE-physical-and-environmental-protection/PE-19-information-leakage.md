# PE-19 — Information Leakage

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-19](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a311pe19).

## Control definition

Protect the system from information leakage through electromagnetic emanations.

## Supplemental guidance

Choose controls based on the sensitivity/classification of the system and applicable national emissions/TEMPEST requirements.

## Related controls

From the [official PE-19 entry](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a311pe19), checked 1 November 2026. These are catalogue relationships, not a claim that the linked controls are implemented.

- **Base control:** No explicit related-control list is provided in the source entry.

## Plain-language metaphor

Some machines can unintentionally whisper information through signals; sensitive rooms may need specialized shielding and placement.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This is a facility/hardware concern, not an application feature. Organisations handling information subject to emissions controls should consult qualified authorities and use approved facilities/equipment.

## Enhancements

1. **PE-19(1), national emissions/TEMPEST policies:** protect components, communications, and networks under applicable national policies based on information sensitivity.
