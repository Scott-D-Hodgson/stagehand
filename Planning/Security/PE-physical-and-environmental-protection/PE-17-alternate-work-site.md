# PE-17 — Alternate Work Site

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-17](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a311pe17).

## Control definition

Apply defined security controls at alternate work sites, assess them as feasible, and provide a way for employees to contact security personnel about incidents or problems.

**Organisation-defined parameter:** controls for alternate-site types.

## Supplemental guidance

Alternate sites include government facilities or employee residences and may support continuity. Controls can vary with the work performed and site risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

Working from another location still requires the locks and safe habits needed at the main office.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

For home/alternate use, require managed devices, secure networks, privacy protections for visible browser sessions, and incident contacts. Do not run sensitive automation from a site lacking required safeguards.
