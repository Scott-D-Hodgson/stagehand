# PE-15 — Water Damage Protection

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-15](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Protect systems from water leaks using accessible, functional master shutoff or isolation valves known to key personnel.

## Supplemental guidance

Primarily applies to server rooms/data centres. Isolation valves may protect specific risk areas without shutting off an entire facility.

## Plain-language metaphor

Know where the water shutoff is before a leak reaches the equipment.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This is a facility/provider control. Operators should verify water protection and incident notification for hosted infrastructure; local workstations inherit building safeguards.

## Enhancements

1. **PE-15(1), automation support:** detect nearby water and alert defined personnel automatically.
