# PE-13 — Fire Protection

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-13](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a311pe13).

## Control definition

Maintain fire detection and suppression systems for the information system, supported by an independent energy source.

## Supplemental guidance

Primarily applies to concentrated computing facilities. Devices can include sprinklers, extinguishers, hoses, and smoke detectors.

## Plain-language metaphor

Equip the machine room with working smoke alarms and suppression systems that still work if normal power fails.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This is a facility/provider responsibility. Operators should review data-centre fire protection and evidence for hosted services; local workstation users should follow building evacuation procedures.

## Enhancements

1. **PE-13(1), detection:** automatically detect fire and notify designated staff/responders.
2. **PE-13(2), suppression:** automatically notify designated staff/responders when suppression activates.
3. **PE-13(3), automatic suppression:** provide automatic fire suppression where facilities are not continuously staffed.
4. **PE-13(4), inspections:** conduct qualified inspections at defined intervals and resolve findings within a defined time.
