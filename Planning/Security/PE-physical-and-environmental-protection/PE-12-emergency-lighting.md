# PE-12 — Emergency Lighting

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-12](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Maintain automatic emergency lighting for system facilities that activates during a power outage and illuminates exits and evacuation routes.

## Supplemental guidance

Primarily applies to data centres, server rooms, and facilities with concentrations of system resources.

## Plain-language metaphor

When the lights fail, the exit path should still be visible so people can leave safely.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This is an inherited facility control. Verify through facility/provider assurance; Stagehand cannot implement emergency lighting.

## Enhancements

1. **PE-12(1), essential missions/business functions:** provide emergency lighting in all facility areas supporting essential functions.
