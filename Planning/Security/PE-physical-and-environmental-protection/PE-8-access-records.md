# PE-8 — Access Records

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-8](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Maintain visitor records for a defined period and review them at a defined frequency.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** retention period and review frequency.

## Supplemental guidance

Visitor records may include identity/organisation, signature or ID, dates/times, visit purpose, and host. The control does not require records for publicly accessible areas.

## Plain-language metaphor

Keep a sign-in sheet showing who visited, when, and whom they met, then review it regularly.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This is a facility responsibility for local offices or data centres, not an application feature. Hosting operators should define record access, retention, and privacy safeguards; users should escort visitors near unattended automation endpoints.

## Enhancements

2. **PE-8(2), physical access records:** withdrawn and incorporated into PE-2.
