# PE-14 — Temperature and Humidity Controls

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-14](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Maintain facility temperature and humidity within defined acceptable levels and monitor them at a defined frequency.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** acceptable ranges and monitoring frequency. **Suggestion for review:** use equipment-manufacturer and facility standards, with continuous monitoring for critical server rooms.

## Supplemental guidance

Primarily applies to facilities with concentrated IT equipment; limits depend on equipment and facility requirements.

## Plain-language metaphor

Keep the server room in a safe climate, like keeping sensitive equipment within its operating temperature range.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This is inherited from the endpoint facility or hosting provider. Request evidence of environmental monitoring and alarm response for hosted runners.

## Enhancements

1. **PE-14(1), automatic controls:** automatically maintain stable temperature/humidity.
2. **PE-14(2), alarms/notifications:** alert on conditions that may harm people or equipment.
