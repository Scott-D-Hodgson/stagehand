# PE-18 — Location of Information System Components

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-18](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Place system components to reduce damage from defined physical/environmental hazards and limit unauthorized access opportunities.

**Organisation-defined parameter:** hazards considered in placement.

## Supplemental guidance

Consider natural hazards, vandalism, electromagnetic interference, and nearby entry points that may enable observation or interception.

## Plain-language metaphor

Do not put the control panel beside a flood-prone door or where passersby can listen in.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Operators/provider should place hosts and network components in suitable secure areas, away from relevant hazards and unauthorized observation. Users should position screens to prevent shoulder surfing.

## Enhancements

1. **PE-18(1), facility site:** select or mitigate a facility location based on physical/environmental hazard assessment.
