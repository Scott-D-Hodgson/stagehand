# PE-4 — Access Control for Transmission Medium

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-4](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a311pe4).

## Control definition

Apply defined physical safeguards to distribution and transmission lines within organisational facilities.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** lines and safeguards.

## Supplemental guidance

Physical controls prevent damage, disruption, tampering, and possible eavesdropping on unencrypted communications. Examples include locked wiring closets, secured spare jacks, and protected cable routes.

## Plain-language metaphor

Protect the wires behind the walls so nobody can cut, tap, or casually plug into them.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

This is an inherited facility/network responsibility. Operators should rely on managed network facilities, protect local cabling and network ports, and use encryption for Stagehand traffic. Confirm safeguards with hosting providers.
