# PE-20 — Asset Monitoring and Tracking

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-20](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a311pe20).

## Control definition

Use defined location technologies to track assets within defined controlled areas and ensure those technologies comply with applicable privacy and government requirements.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** asset scope, technology, and controlled areas.

## Supplemental guidance

Tracking can help keep critical assets in authorized locations. Consult legal and privacy officials because location data can affect individual privacy.

## Plain-language metaphor

Use an inventory tag to find important equipment, while setting clear limits on who can track it and why.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand does not track physical asset locations. Organisations may use endpoint/device-management inventory for laptops and servers, with privacy review, access restriction, and defined retention.
