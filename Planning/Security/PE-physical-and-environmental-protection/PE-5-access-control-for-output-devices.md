# PE-5 — Access Control for Output Devices

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-5](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a311pe5).

## Control definition

Control physical access to output devices so unauthorized people cannot obtain system output.

## Supplemental guidance

Output devices include monitors, printers, copiers, scanners, fax machines, and audio devices. Place them in controlled/observable locations and limit access to output.

## Plain-language metaphor

Keep printouts and screens where only intended recipients can see or collect them.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Screenshots, reports, and printed test output may contain sensitive data. Users should lock screens, collect printouts promptly, and use secure-release printing where available. Avoid public displays during automation.

## Enhancements

1. **PE-5(1), authorized recipients:** control output and ensure only authorized people receive it.
2. **PE-5(2), identity-linked output:** link a recipient’s identity to release of output.
3. **PE-5(3), marking output devices:** label devices to indicate the sensitivity of information they may output.
