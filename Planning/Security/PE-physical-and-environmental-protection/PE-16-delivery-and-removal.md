# PE-16 — Delivery and Removal

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-16](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a311pe16).

## Control definition

Authorize, monitor, and control defined system components entering or leaving a facility and maintain records of those movements.

**Organisation-defined parameter:** component types subject to entry/removal controls.

## Supplemental guidance

Secure delivery areas and consider isolating them from system/media spaces to enforce authorization.

## Plain-language metaphor

Record equipment arriving and leaving, like checking tools in and out of a secure workshop.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Track laptops, storage media, and server components used for Stagehand deployments. Operators should authorize removal/repair, record serials and custody, and sanitize storage before transfer.
