# PE-1 — Physical and Environmental Protection Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Develop and distribute physical/environmental protection policy and procedures to defined roles; address purpose, scope, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance; review/update them on defined schedules.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipients and review frequencies. **Suggestion for review:** include facility, system, and security owners; review annually and after facility or system changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy implements the PE family, aligns with applicable government requirements, and may be enterprise-wide with system-specific procedures based on risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

This is the building’s safety rulebook: who may enter, how equipment is protected, and what to do about fire, power, or water.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand cannot secure its users’ facilities. Operators should apply the organisation’s physical/environmental program to workstations, data centres, and any hosted infrastructure, and obtain provider evidence for cloud-hosted components.
