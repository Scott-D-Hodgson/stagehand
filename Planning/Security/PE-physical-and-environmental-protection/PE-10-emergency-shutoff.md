# PE-10 — Emergency Shutoff

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PE-10](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Provide emergency power shutoff for the system/components, locate switches where personnel can reach them safely, and protect against unauthorized activation.

**Organisation-defined parameter:** location of shutoff switches/devices.

## Supplemental guidance

Primarily applies to facilities with concentrated computing resources such as data centres and server rooms.

## Plain-language metaphor

Place a clearly reachable emergency stop near the machine, but guard it against accidental or unauthorized use.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Physical emergency shutoff is a facility control. For local deployments, users should know safe host shutdown procedures. Data-centre operators should define authorized activation and protect switches while preserving safety.

## Enhancements

1. **PE-10(1), accidental/unauthorized activation:** withdrawn and incorporated into PE-10 base control.
