# IA-10 — Adaptive Identification and Authentication

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IA-10](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a37ia10).

## Control definition

Require defined supplemental authentication techniques when defined circumstances occur.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** additional methods and triggering conditions.

## Supplemental guidance

If activity suggests an account may be compromised—such as unusual data volume, access outside normal duties, or suspicious network origin—the organisation may require stronger or additional authentication.

## Plain-language metaphor

If a visitor asks for access to a restricted room, ask for a second check even if their first pass looks valid.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Adaptive decisions belong to the organisation’s identity and target systems. Operators can require reauthentication or human confirmation for high-risk actions, unusual destinations, or bulk access. A test plan cannot authorize suspicious activity or bypass the organisation's identity policy.
