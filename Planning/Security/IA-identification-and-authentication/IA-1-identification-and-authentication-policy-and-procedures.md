# IA-1 — Identification and Authentication Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, IA-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute identification/authentication policy and procedures to defined roles; cover purpose, scope, accountability, management commitment, coordination, and compliance; and review/update them at defined intervals.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipients and review frequencies. **Suggestion for review:** distribute to identity administrators, system owners, security personnel, and affected users; review annually and after significant identity-system changes.

## Supplemental guidance

These documents implement the IA family and should align with applicable government policy. Enterprise policy may cover multiple systems, with system-specific procedures added where risk requires.

## Plain-language metaphor

This is the organisation’s rulebook for issuing and checking keys: who gets one, how it is checked, and how the rules stay current.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand should rely on approved identity services and avoid embedding its own undocumented authentication policy. Operators should define identity proofing, authentication methods, recovery, and access review for their host, browser, CI, and target-service accounts. This is planned-state guidance, not implementation evidence.
