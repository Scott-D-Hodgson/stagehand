# PL-9 — Central Management

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PL-9](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a312pl9).

## Control definition

Centrally manage organisation-defined security controls and related processes.

**Organisation-defined parameter:** controls/processes selected for central management.

## Supplemental guidance

Central management can include planning, implementing, assessing, authorizing, and monitoring common controls. It can standardize implementation and conserve resources. Some controls may be hybrid where only part can be managed centrally.

## Plain-language metaphor

Set shared building-wide rules from one control room, while leaving room-specific tasks to local operators.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Organisations may centrally manage identity, endpoint baselines, logs, browser policies, and approved runner images. Identify which safeguards are common, inherited, or system-specific; centrally managed services must still be evidenced for this deployment.
