# PL-1 — Security Planning Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PL-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a312pl1).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute security-planning policy/procedures to defined roles; address purpose, scope, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance; and review/update both on defined schedules.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipients and review frequency. **Suggestion for review:** include system owners, security authorities, and project leaders; review annually and after significant system or policy changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy supports PL-family controls, reflects applicable government requirements, and may be enterprise-wide with system-specific procedures based on risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

This is the planning rulebook for deciding what security paperwork and decisions are needed before a system operates.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Product maintainers should document planned architecture and security assumptions. Operators must integrate Stagehand into their own system-planning and authorization processes; these files do not replace a deployment security plan.
