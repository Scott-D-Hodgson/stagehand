# PL-4 — Rules of Behaviour

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PL-4](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a312pl4).

## Control definition

Make rules for system and information use available to users; obtain acknowledgement that they read, understand, and agree before granting access; review rules at a defined frequency; and require acknowledgement of revised rules.

**Organisation-defined parameter:** review frequency. **Suggestion for review:** annually and after significant use-policy changes.

## Supplemental guidance

Rules can vary by role (e.g., administrators versus general users). Training that includes the rules may satisfy acknowledgement requirements; electronic acknowledgements may be used. AC-8 may cover some non-organisational users.

## Plain-language metaphor

Before borrowing a powerful tool, read and accept the workshop rules; sign again when the rules change.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Organisations should state permitted Stagehand use, data restrictions, human review expectations, prohibited destinations/actions, credential handling, and incident reporting. Require users and administrators to acknowledge these rules before access, and refresh after material changes.

## Enhancements

1. **PL-4(1), social media/networking restrictions:** explicitly restrict social-media use and posting organisational information publicly, including protections against disclosure or inference of non-public information.
