# SA-1 - System and Services Acquisition Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SA-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute acquisition policy and procedures to assigned roles; review and update both on defined schedules. The policy addresses purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** recipients and review frequency. **Suggestion for review:** include procurement, system owners, security, and contracting roles; review annually and after major policy changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy and procedures implement selected SA controls and reflect applicable government requirements. They may be organisation-wide or system-specific, guided by risk.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like a purchasing rulebook that makes sure safety requirements are considered before buying or building.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand maintainers should document security requirements in development and acquisition processes. Deploying organisations should apply their procurement and service oversight policy to hosting, CI, browsers, and support services.
