# SA-16 - Developer Provided Training

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SA-16](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a315sa16).

## Control definition

Require the developer to provide defined training on correct use and operation of implemented security functions, controls, and mechanisms.

**Organisation-defined parameter:** training. **Suggestion for review:** provide role-based admin and user guidance when security features are introduced or materially changed; maintain reusable training material.

## Supplemental guidance

Applies to internal and external developers. Training supports effective use of implemented controls and can be classroom, online, hands-on, or materials for organisation-run training. Different security functions may need different training.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like teaching people how to use the safety equipment before they operate the machine.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Maintainers should provide secure-operation guidance for the runner, plans, secrets, and output artifacts as these features are implemented. Operators should train staff on safe target selection, credential handling, artifact review, and environment configuration.
