# SA-21 - Developer Screening

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SA-21](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Require developers of assigned systems/components/services to hold access authorizations appropriate to assigned official duties and meet additional personnel-screening criteria.

**Organisation-defined parameters:** systems/services in scope, duties, and screening criteria. **Suggestion for review:** select criteria through the organisation’s security authority and applicable screening rules; do not impose nationality or clearance criteria without an applicable requirement.

## Supplemental guidance

Developer trust may need to match the trust level expected of deployed-system users where the system supports critical national/economic activities. Criteria can include clearance or background checks; supplier ownership/relationships may also affect confidence in reliability.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like checking that the people building a secure facility have the authorization required for the rooms and equipment they handle.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand does not screen contributors. Maintainers or procuring organisations define applicable access/screening requirements based on system sensitivity and legal obligations, and document provider evidence where required.

## Control enhancements

(1) require developers to take defined steps to validate that required authorizations and screening are satisfied, for example by supplying an authorized-developer list for organisational verification.

## Assessment status

**Planned / organisation-dependent; not verified.** No developer screening evidence is asserted.
