# SA-2 - Allocation of Resources

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SA-2](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a315sa2).

## Control definition

Determine security control needs during mission/business planning; document and allocate the resources to protect the system/service through capital planning; and include a distinct information-security budget line.

No organisation-defined parameters are stated in the base control.

## Supplemental guidance

Resources include both initial acquisition and ongoing sustainment of the system or service.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like budgeting for both the fire alarm installation and its future inspections.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Product maintainers should plan resources for secure development, dependency maintenance, release integrity, and vulnerability response. Operators budget for secure hosting, identity, monitoring, backups, and assessments; these are not supplied by the Stagehand app.
