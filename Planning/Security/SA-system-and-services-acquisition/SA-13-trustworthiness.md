# SA-13 - Trustworthiness

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, SA-13](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a315sa13).

## Control definition

Annex 3A records SA-13 as not allocated in Government of Canada profiles. It notes that CSE’s definition of robustness is synonymous with NIST’s definition of trustworthiness and refers readers to ITSG-33 Annex 2, section 7, for robustness within the ISSIP. Annex 3A provides no separate control statement or enhancements here.

## Supplemental guidance

The catalogue entry is an allocation note and cross-reference, not a base control with separate supplemental guidance.

## Related controls

From the [official SA-13 entry](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a315sa13), checked 1 November 2026. These are catalogue relationships, not a claim that the linked controls are implemented.

- **Base control:** No explicit related-control list is provided in the source entry.

## Plain-language metaphor

This entry points to the broader process for deciding how much confidence a system deserves; it is not a separate checklist item in the GC profiles.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Do not claim SA-13 as an allocated GC profile requirement solely from this Annex entry. The organisation should address robustness/trustworthiness through its applicable risk-management process and selected controls. Stagehand maintainers can support that process with design, testing, and supply-chain evidence as it becomes available.
