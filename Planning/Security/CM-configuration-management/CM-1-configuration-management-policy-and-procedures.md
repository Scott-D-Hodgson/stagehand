# CM-1 — Configuration Management Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, CM-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Develop and distribute configuration-management policy and procedures to defined roles. Address purpose, scope, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance; review and update both at defined intervals.

**Organisation-defined parameters to review:** recipients and review frequencies. **Suggested values for review:** include system owners, developers, release managers, and administrators; review annually and after significant organisational or technical changes.

## Supplemental guidance

The policy and procedures implement the CM family and should reflect applicable legislation and government direction. Enterprise policy may cover multiple systems, with system-specific procedures added based on risk.

## In plain language

This is the organisation’s rulebook for changing systems safely: who may change them, how changes are checked, and how the rules stay current.

## Stagehand initial assessment

The maintainers should define product configuration items and change processes across plans/schema, source, dependencies, builds, releases, and documentation. Operators should establish their own configuration procedures for the host, browser, runner, and policy settings. The presence of version control alone does not establish a complete CM program.
