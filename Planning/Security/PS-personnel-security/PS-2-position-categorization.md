# PS-2 — Position Categorization

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PS-2](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a313ps2).

## Control definition

Categorize positions according to potential harm from malicious misuse of their privileges, select the appropriate screening level, and review the categorization periodically.

Suggested review: annually and whenever duties or privileges materially change.

## Supplemental guidance

Screening levels should follow the applicable TBS Standard on Security Screening; this is an organisational decision.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like deciding which jobs need a basic key, a master key, or a guarded vault key.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand cannot assign positions or conduct screening. Operators should categorize maintainers, CI administrators, and people with access to sensitive test data under their organisational process.
