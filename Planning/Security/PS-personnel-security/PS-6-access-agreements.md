# PS-6 — Access Agreements

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PS-6](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a313ps6).

## Control definition

Create and maintain access agreements; require users to sign before access and again after updates or at a defined interval.

Suggested review: annual review and re-acknowledgment after material changes.

## Supplemental guidance

Agreements may include nondisclosure, acceptable-use, rules-of-behaviour, or conflict-of-interest terms; electronic signatures may be used when permitted.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like reading and signing the rules before receiving a building pass.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand does not issue agreements. Operators should cover handling of plans, credentials, screenshots, traces, and generated reports in their onboarding and acceptable-use agreements.

## Control enhancements

Enhancements: (1) withdrawn and incorporated into PS-3; (2) special-protection classified information requires official-duty authorization, screening, and signed nondisclosure terms; (3) communicate legally binding post-employment duties and obtain acknowledgment where applicable.
