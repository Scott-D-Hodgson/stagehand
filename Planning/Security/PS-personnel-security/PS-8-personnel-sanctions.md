# PS-8 — Personnel Sanctions

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PS-8](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a313ps8).

## Control definition

Use a formal process for people who violate information-security rules and notify defined roles within a defined time, identifying the person and reason as authorized.

Suggested review: promptly route cases to HR, security, and legal under restricted handling.

## Supplemental guidance

Sanctions must reflect applicable law and policy; legal counsel should advise. Access agreements can describe the process.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like having known consequences when someone ignores safety rules.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand does not administer sanctions. Each operator and maintainer organisation owns the process and should avoid putting sensitive personnel details in application logs.
