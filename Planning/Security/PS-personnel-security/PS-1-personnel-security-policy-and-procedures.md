# PS-1 — Personnel Security Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, PS-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Define and distribute personnel-security policy and procedures to assigned roles; review and update them on specified schedules.

Suggested review: provide to HR, security, managers, and system owners; review annually and after significant change.

## Supplemental guidance

Explains GC personnel-security requirements and supports the selected PS controls. It may be enterprise-wide with system-specific procedures as needed.

## Plain-language metaphor

Like a workplace rulebook for who may be trusted with keys and what happens when roles change.

## Stagehand and operator guidance

Stagehand has no personnel-management service. Maintainers and each deploying organisation must apply its own personnel policies to repository, build, and runtime access.

## Control enhancements

None listed.

## Assessment status

**Planned / not verified.** Stagehand implementation and deployment evidence must be assessed before assigning an implemented or compliant status. This summary is a paraphrase for planning, not an authorization decision.
