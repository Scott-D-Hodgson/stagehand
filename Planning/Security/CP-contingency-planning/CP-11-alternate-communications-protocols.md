# CP-11 — Alternate Communications Protocols

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, CP-11](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a36cp11).

## Control definition

Provide the capability to use organisation-defined alternative communication protocols to maintain continuity of operations.

**Organisation-defined parameter to review:** approved alternative protocols and conditions for use.

## Supplemental guidance

Contingency plans, training, and tests should include alternate protocol capabilities where needed for resilience. Analyse side effects on applications before switching protocols.

## In plain language

If the usual road is closed, identify a safe alternate route and check that the vehicles and directions still work on it.

## Stagehand initial assessment

Most deployments should use approved network failover rather than ad hoc protocol changes. Where alternative connectivity is needed, operators should test compatibility, security, and logging in advance and maintain approved settings. No protocol-switching capability is claimed for Stagehand.
