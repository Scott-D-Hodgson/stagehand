# CP-6 — Alternate Storage Site

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, CP-6](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Establish an alternate site and agreements for storing and retrieving system backups, and ensure its safeguards are equivalent to the primary site.

## Supplemental guidance

Alternate sites are geographically distinct and hold duplicate information/data. Agreements address conditions, access, physical/environmental protections, and delivery/retrieval of backup media. Site choices should support continuity requirements.

## In plain language

Keep a protected spare copy somewhere far enough away that the same local disaster is unlikely to destroy both copies.

## Stagehand initial assessment

Operators should decide whether plans, configuration, logs, or reports require backup, classify them, and select approved geographically separated storage with equivalent access and encryption safeguards. Test retrieval and ensure backups do not preserve credentials or sensitive browser artifacts unnecessarily.

## Control enhancements

1. **CP-6(1), separation from primary:** separate sites against the same risk scenarios identified by the organisation.
2. **CP-6(2), recovery objectives:** configure the alternate site to meet recovery-time and recovery-point objectives.
3. **CP-6(3), accessibility:** identify access barriers during area-wide disruption and define mitigations.
