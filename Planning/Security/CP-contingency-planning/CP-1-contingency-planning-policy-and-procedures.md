# CP-1 — Contingency Planning Policy and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, CP-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a36cp1).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute a contingency-planning policy and procedures to defined roles; review and update both on defined schedules; and establish an audit cycle for the contingency-plan program as a basis for regular reporting to TBS.

**Organisation-defined parameters to review:** recipients and policy/procedure review frequency. **Suggested values for review:** include system owners, continuity leads, incident responders, and administrators; review annually and after exercises or significant changes.

## Supplemental guidance

Contingency policies support the CP family and should align with applicable government requirements. Organisation-wide policy may be sufficient, supplemented by system-specific procedures where risk requires.

## In plain language

This is the rulebook for what to do when normal operations fail, including who plans, who acts, and how the plan gets checked.
