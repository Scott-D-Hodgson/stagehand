# AT-4 — Security Training Records

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, AT-4](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Document and monitor each person’s security training, including general awareness and system-specific training, and retain individual records for an organisation-defined period.

**Organisation-defined parameter to review:** record-retention period. **Suggested value for review:** retain for the duration of access/engagement and the organisation’s applicable audit and records-retention period; confirm this with records-management and privacy owners rather than applying a universal period.

## Supplemental guidance

Organisations may allow supervisors to maintain records for specialized training. Records should support evidence that required training occurred while being handled under privacy and records-management requirements.

## In plain language

Keep a sign-in sheet for safety instruction so the organisation can tell who has been trained and who still needs a session.

## Stagehand initial assessment

Stagehand does not establish or retain workforce training records as part of this assessment. The adopting organisation should record completion of required Stagehand-specific training in its approved learning or personnel system, restrict access to those records, and apply its retention schedule. Avoid placing personal training data in Stagehand logs or project artifacts.

## Control enhancements

None are listed for AT-4 in Annex 3A.
