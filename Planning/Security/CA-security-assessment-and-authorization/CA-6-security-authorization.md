# CA-6 — Security Authorization

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, CA-6](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Assign a senior executive/manager as authorizing official; obtain authorization before processing begins; and update the authorization at a defined frequency.

**Organisation-defined parameter to review:** authorization update frequency. **Suggested value for review:** use ongoing authorization supported by continuous monitoring and revisit authorization after significant risk, scope, or architecture changes.

## Supplemental guidance

Authorization is a formal management decision accepting residual risk based on agreed controls. The official must have authority commensurate with the risk and accountability for operation. Government policy may require an appropriate government official for government systems. Continuous monitoring can support ongoing authorization and reduce duplicate reauthorization effort.

## In plain language

An accountable decision-maker signs off that a system may operate after reviewing its safeguards and remaining risks.

## Stagehand initial assessment

Stagehand cannot authorize itself or accept the adopting organisation’s risk. The system owner should define the authorization boundary and obtain approval before operational use where required. Keep authorization evidence current as the product and deployment evolve; distinguish vendor/product evidence from inherited or locally implemented controls.
