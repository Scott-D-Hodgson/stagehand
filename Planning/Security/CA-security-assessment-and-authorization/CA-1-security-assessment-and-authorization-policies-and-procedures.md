# CA-1 — Security Assessment and Authorization Policies and Procedures

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, CA-1](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33).

## Control definition

Develop, document, and distribute security assessment and authorization policy and procedures to organisation-defined roles. Cover purpose, scope, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance; review and update both on defined schedules.

**Organisation-defined parameters to review:** recipients and policy/procedure review frequency. **Suggested values for review:** distribute to system owners, authorizing officials, security assessors, and operators; review annually and after material policy changes.

## Supplemental guidance

Policies and procedures support effective implementation of the CA family, reflect applicable legislation and government direction, and can be enterprise-wide or system-specific according to risk.

## In plain language

This is the playbook for checking a system’s safety and deciding who may accept its risks.

## Stagehand initial assessment

Stagehand documentation can describe product capabilities and evidence, but authorization policy and decisions belong to the adopting organisation. Include the application, runner, browser, identity, target systems, and hosting environment in the assessment boundary. Use the organisation’s established security authorization process.
