# CA-5 — Plan of Action and Milestones

**Source:** [ITSG-33 Annex 3A, CA-5](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a34ca5).

## Control definition

Maintain a plan documenting corrective actions for assessment weaknesses and known vulnerabilities; update it on a defined schedule using assessment, security-impact, and monitoring findings.

**Organisation-defined parameter to review:** update frequency. **Suggested value for review:** update when a finding changes and review monthly for open high-risk items, with the organisation setting its formal cadence.

## Supplemental guidance

Plans of action and milestones are core parts of security operations and authorization packages and may support organisational or government reporting.

## In plain language

It is a repair list with owners and due dates, kept current as new problems are discovered or fixed.
