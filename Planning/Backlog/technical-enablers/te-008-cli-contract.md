**Status:** Proposed  
**Priority:** Must  
**Related stories:** ATR-025, ATR-026, ATR-027

## Objective

Define CLI arguments, override precedence, exit codes, stdout/stderr behaviour, and noninteractive operation.

## Acceptance criteria

- Failure modes and security implications are addressed where relevant.
